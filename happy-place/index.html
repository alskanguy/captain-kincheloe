Welcome to your Happy Place

Start with an empty tunnel, 120 coins, 42 seeds, and six bags of compost. All 90 planting spots are available.

1. Plant. Pick a seed and click an empty spot. Mix crops in any bed.
2. Water. Choose Water bed and click once for the whole bed. Water lasts about 12 garden days.
3. Harvest. Golden rings mark ripe plants. Harvest each plant individually.
4. Sell. Stock your farm stand from your harvest. Adjust the suggested prices; customers buy automatically.

Buy more seeds, bees, ladybugs, amendments, fans, and irrigation in the supply shop. Select a bed to apply amendments. Perennials grow back after harvest.

Time: 15 seconds per day, only while this tab is visible and unpaused. Dry soil pauses growth. Mature crops stay fresh in this first version.

Gentle challenge: Heat and aphids reduce growth and yield slightly. Warnings give you time to buy fans and ladybugs. There is no frost or plant death.
