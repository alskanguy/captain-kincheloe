# My Happy Place: High Tunnel

A self-contained browser gardening game inspired by Steve’s overhead high tunnel map in Homer, Alaska. Open `index.html` in a modern browser; no build or dependencies required.

## Play

- All 12 beds are available: seven 4×12 beds and five 4×4 beds, with 90 gameplay planting spots.
- Start with empty beds, 42 seeds, six compost bags, and 120 coins.
- Pick a seed and click individual spots to plant. Water a whole bed at once. Harvest individual ripe plants marked with golden rings.
- Stock harvested produce at the farm stand. Suggested prices are adjustable from 1–999 coins. Customers visit every five seconds during active play; higher prices reduce purchase probability.
- Buy seeds, bees, ladybugs, amendments, ventilation, and irrigation with crop earnings.
- A garden day lasts 15 seconds. Growth and sales pause when the tab is hidden, paused, or a dialog is open.
- Progress saves in browser local storage. Saves are specific to the browser/device and site origin. New Game requires confirmation.

## Modes

Cozy mode has no heat or pest penalties. Gentle challenge adds modest heat and aphid effects, with fans and ladybugs providing permanent protection. There is no frost or plant death. Dry soil pauses growth.

## First version

The center tree and blueberry row is scenery. Raised-bed crops are playable. The map follows the available overhead planning chart; plant spots and artwork are gameplay representations. Produce does not spoil. Beneficial insects are permanent upgrades. Garden-day fractions reset on reload (up to 15 seconds); no offline advancement occurs.

The original Captain Kincheloe game remains at the repository root.
